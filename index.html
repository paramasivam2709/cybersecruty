Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about Stackly's cybersecurity solutions.

What makes Stackly different from other cybersecurity providers?
Stackly uses AI-driven systems that continuously learn and adapt to new threats, providing proactive defense that evolves daily rather than relying on static rule-based protection.
How quickly can I implement Stackly's security solutions?
Most implementations are completed within 24-48 hours. Our team provides full support during setup, and our AI systems begin learning your environment immediately.
Do you offer compliance support for regulations like GDPR and HIPAA?
Yes, our Enterprise and Pro plans include automated compliance monitoring and reporting for major regulations including GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS.
What kind of support do you provide?
All plans include 24/7 technical support. Enterprise customers receive dedicated account managers and priority incident response. Our AI systems also provide automated alerts and recommendations.
Can Stackly integrate with my existing security tools?
Absolutely. Stackly is designed to complement and enhance existing security infrastructure. We offer APIs and pre-built integrations with popular security tools and platforms.
